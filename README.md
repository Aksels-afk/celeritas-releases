# Celeritas for Windows

Private, on-device meeting transcription. Records Zoom, Teams and Google Meet calls with **no bot in the call**, transcribes them **entirely on your PC**, and turns the conversation into summaries and ready-to-push tasks.

## Download

**[Download the latest installer (Celeritas-Setup.exe)](https://github.com/Aksels-afk/celeritas-releases/releases/latest/download/Celeritas-Setup.exe)** · [All releases](https://github.com/Aksels-afk/celeritas-releases/releases) · [getceleritas.com](https://getceleritas.com)

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

> The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info -> Run anyway**.

This repository only hosts installers. Audio never leaves your computer.
